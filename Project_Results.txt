# Hospital Patient Arrival Analysis

## Project data
- Dataset: synthetic_hospital_arrivals.csv
- Period: 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025
- Number of days: 365
- Data type: Simulated hospital arrivals, not real hospital records

## Descriptive statistics
- Mean daily arrivals: 44.98
- Standard deviation: 9.31
- Minimum: 21
- Median: 45
- Maximum: 69

## Weekday vs weekend comparison
- Weekday count: 261 days
- Weekday mean: 48.93 patients/day
- Weekend count: 104 days
- Weekend mean: 35.05 patients/day
- Difference in means: approximately 13.88 patients/day

## Poisson probability analysis
- Average arrival rate (lambda): 44.98
- Probability of exactly 45 arrivals: 5.94%
- Probability of more than 50 arrivals: 20.27%
- Probability of fewer than 40 arrivals: 20.94%

## Hypothesis testing
- Test: Welch's independent-samples t-test
- Null hypothesis: Weekday and weekend mean arrivals are equal
- Alternative hypothesis: Weekday and weekend mean arrivals differ
- Significance level: 0.05
- t-statistic: 18.7394
- Decision: Reject the null hypothesis
- Interpretation: A statistically significant difference was found in this simulated dataset.

### Forecast Method Comparison

Two forecasting methods were compared using the same 65-day test dataset, after training on the first 300 days.

| Method | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|
| 7-day Moving Average | 7.319 | 9.192 |
| Simple Exponential Smoothing | 7.163 | 8.809 |

**Conclusion:** Simple Exponential Smoothing performed slightly better than the 7-day Moving Average because it achieved lower MAE and RMSE values. Its MAE was approximately 7.16 patients per day.

**Evaluation method:** Rolling one-day-ahead forecasting, using observed arrivals to update predictions for subsequent days.

**Data limitation:** The dataset is simulated and was created for educational demonstration. The findings should not be interpreted as real hospital arrival patterns.
